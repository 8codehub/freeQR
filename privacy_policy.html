QR Studio Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026. Applies to version 3.0.

On-device creation

QR Studio generates QR codes and renders designs on your device. The active application includes no account system, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service. Its Android application requests no network or camera permission. QR content and usage information are not sent to the developer.

Drafts and selected photos

The app stores your current QR content, design layers and theme locally using DataStore. Android saved instance state also helps restore an interrupted session. Photos you choose through the system photo picker are copied to app-private cache for local editing. The operating system may clear cached images, requiring you to select the photo again. Drafts can contain sensitive content, including Wi-Fi passwords. Android application backup is disabled.

Saving and sharing

Save exports a PNG to your device photo library. Android 8 and 9 request storage permission to save; newer Android versions use MediaStore. On iOS, the app requests permission to add the exported image to Photos. Share passes the exported PNG to the app you choose through the system share sheet, using an app-private temporary file. Receiving apps handle the shared image under their own policies. Anyone who scans an exported QR can read its encoded content.

Deletion

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its local drafts, preferences and cached images. Exported or shared copies remain outside the app and can be deleted with your photo library, file manager or receiving app.

Previous versions

Earlier versions included Firebase Analytics. Version 3.0 does not include that integration. This policy describes the current version and does not change how previous versions handled data.
